Sew-Out Tracker

How this works (30-second read)
  • Log once, then click to advance. Add a request with the bar below. After that, every button on a card stamps the time automatically — those clicks are your log.
  • The red strip up top is your "do this now" list — overdue SLAs, with the exact next action (e.g. follow up with Barrett + cc Renée).
  • SLA clocks: 48 hrs to get a request to Barrett · 24 hrs for Barrett to send the sew-out (else follow up) · forward to the rep the same day it's received.
  • Nothing is deleted. Completed requests leave the board but stay in History — pick any past date to see what happened.
  • Report tab writes your morning + end-of-day messages from the timestamps. Export saves a JSON backup to OneDrive.
Tip: fill Account + Logo and press Enter to log fast. Everything else is optional.
